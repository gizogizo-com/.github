
# GIZOGIZO

**GIZOGIZO.COM LTD** is a Nigerian technology company focused on building practical, reliable, and scalable digital solutions for businesses, organizations, and communities.

### What We Do

Our areas of focus include:

- **Software Engineering** — Custom software applications and digital platforms.
- **Web & Mobile Development** — Modern applications for businesses and organizations.
- **Digital Commerce** — Technology connecting buyers, sellers, and businesses.
- **IT Consulting** — Technology advisory, systems integration, and digital transformation.

### Our Flagship Initiative

**GIZOGIZO Marketplace** is a digital commerce platform under development, designed to connect buyers, sellers, local businesses, and traditional markets across Nigeria.

### About the Company

GIZOGIZO was first registered as **GIZOGIZO.COM TECHNOLOGY** in 2024 and subsequently incorporated as **GIZOGIZO.COM LTD** in 2026.
